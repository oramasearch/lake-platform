A trademark tells people who a product comes from. This policy protects that and nothing more. It

## What you may do without asking

- **Say what is true.** For example: "runs on Orama Lake", "a fork of Orama Lake", "compatible
  with Orama Lake", "we offer support for Orama Lake". Use the plain word mark in text, not a logo.
- **Pass on an official release unchanged**, under its own name, with its license and notices.
- **Write and talk about it**: articles, talks, courses, books, reviews and comparisons.
- **Run community activity** such as a meetup, a chat group or a user group, as long as its name
  or description makes clear it is not run by Orama (for example "Berlin Orama Lake users,
  unofficial").
- **Use the marks inside your own organization**, for example in a private deployment.

## What needs a different name

- **A fork or a changed version you distribute.** Give it its own name and its own logo, ones that
  people will not confuse with the marks. You may say it is "based on Orama Lake". Take the Orama
  logos out of the user interface, the documentation and the packages.
- **A product, service, company or domain name.** Do not use a mark, or something that looks or
  sounds like one, as part of it. "Acme Lake Platform, built on Orama Lake" is fine; "Orama Lake
  Cloud" or "lakelive-hosting.com" is not.
- **A hosted or managed service.** You may run Orama Lake for others and say it is built on Orama
  Lake. The service needs its own name, and it must not suggest that Orama runs, endorses or
  certifies it.

## What you may not do

- Suggest that Orama made, endorses, certifies or supports your product, service or event when it
  does not.
- Change a logo, combine it with your own mark, or use it in a way that makes it hard to read.
- Use a mark in a way that is false, misleading or harmful to Orama's reputation.

For anything this page does not cover, or to ask for permission, write to
**trademarks@orama.com**. OramaSearch, Inc. may update this policy; the version in this repository
is the current one.
